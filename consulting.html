Our approach

Ads win the shelf. Organic builds the brand.

Most brands get stuck paying for every sale. We use ads to win rank, then let organic sales carry the product.

STAGE 1

Launch

Fix the product page first. Then use ads to put the product in front of the right shoppers on the right search terms.

STAGE 2

Rank

Push the winners up the category rankings with ads, deals and reviews, and keep stock in the cities where orders come from.

STAGE 3

Sustain

Once the rank holds, organic sales take over. We cut ads back to the terms worth defending and grow profit.

The number we watch: TACoS, ad spend divided by total sales. If it falls month after month, your brand is growing on its own.

FAQ

Questions brands ask us

Who do you work with?

Manufacturers, distributors and brand owners in food, FMCG, wellness, Ayurveda and beverages, and restaurants that want packaged or online sales. Both brands that are not online yet and brands already online but not growing.

We're not online yet. Where do we start?

With the Growth Audit. We check your products, pricing and margins, choose the right platforms to start with, and give you a launch plan with clear steps.

We already sell online, but sales are flat. Can you help?

Yes, this is most of our work. We usually find the gap in one of a few places: search visibility, the product page, stock in the right cities, price per unit, or ad spend that doesn't pay back.

Do you run our ads and listings?

Both ways work. In Growth Advisory your team or agency executes and we set the strategy and review the work. In Managed Growth our team runs execution.

How do you charge?

The Growth Audit is a one-time fee. Advisory is a fixed monthly retainer. Managed Growth is a fixed fee plus a share of the growth. We share exact fees after the free growth check, once we know your size and goals.

Is my data safe?

Yes. We sign an NDA before we see any sales or ads data, and we never share one client's data with another.
