00Rules prompt (paste first)

AI SKILLS FOR MARKETPLACES & QUICK COMMERCE: RULES
By Growth Labs AI | egrowthlabsconsulting@gmail.com

You are my senior e-commerce analyst. I sell a consumer brand on marketplaces and quick-commerce apps.
My markets: [India / GCC / Both]. My platforms: [list]. My currency: [INR / SAR / AED].

For every skill I paste:
1. Use only the data I give you. Never invent a number, rating, rank, fee, competitor fact or platform rule. If something is missing, say "Not in the data" and ask.
2. Start with "What I received": rows, columns, date range, and anything that looks wrong.
3. Lead with the answer. Use tables for lists. Show each formula once.
4. Sort actions by money impact, biggest first.
5. End with "Your call": the decisions only I should make.
6. When you rely on a platform rule, tell me to confirm it in the platform's own help pages.
7. English only.

Reply "Ready." and wait for the first skill.
Skills 1–5

Listings

Get found and win the click.

01Listing Audit

SKILL 1: LISTING AUDIT

Audit this listing like a senior marketplace manager before a big sale.
Platform: [e.g. Amazon]   Listing text: [paste title, bullets, description]
Images: [count]   Video: [yes/no]   Rating and reviews: [ ]

Score it out of 100: Title 20, Images 25, Bullets 20, Description/A+ 15, Search terms 10, Trust 10.
Check the title shows brand, product, variant and pack size in the first 60 characters.
Give a ranked fix list: Fix | Where | Why | Effort.
Rewrite the title and the 2 weakest bullets using only facts I gave you.

028-Image Set

SKILL 2: 8-IMAGE SET

Use in ChatGPT, Gemini or Copilot (they make images). Attach 1 product photo.
Title: [paste]   Market: [India / GCC / Both]

First reply, no images: read the pack (brand, variant, size, colours, claims printed on it), list facts to confirm, list the top 3 shopper doubts, lock one style (pack colours, one font, max 25 words per image), and plan 8 images: Main on white, Why buy, What's inside, Size, In use, How to use, Why us, Value.
Then make ONE image each time I type "next". Keep the pack exactly as in my photo. Never invent text, badges or ratings. After each image, check: pack matches, spelling, readable on a phone.

03A+ Plan

SKILL 3: A+ CONTENT PLAN

Plan the brand content section below the listing (Amazon A+, or the platform's equivalent).
Product: [name]   Facts I can prove: [list]   Top 3 shopper doubts: [list]   Other products in my range: [list]

Plan 5 modules, top to bottom: brand promise, the 3 benefits, how it works or what's inside, comparison with my own range (never a competitor), and a closing reassurance.
For each module: Module | Job | Headline (max 6 words) | Body (max 30 words) | Image brief.
Remind me to check the platform's current module sizes and rules.

04Keyword Gaps

SKILL 4: KEYWORD GAPS

Find search terms that sell but are missing from my listing.
Attach: my search term report (30-60 days). Paste: my title and bullets.

List terms with 2 or more orders. Mark each: IN TITLE / IN BULLETS / MISSING.
For MISSING terms, sorted by orders: Term | Orders | Sales | Where to add it | Natural sentence using it.
Never suggest stuffing. Flag competitor brand names: do not add them to my listing.

05Regional Copy Check

SKILL 5: REGIONAL COPY CHECK

Check my listing in another language (Hindi, Arabic or any other) against my English one.
Paste: English title and bullets, then the translated title and bullets. Language and market: [ ]

Table: Line | Meaning matches? | Missing or added facts | Tone (natural / literal translation) | Suggested fix.
Flag numbers, units and pack sizes that differ between the two versions.
Write in English, quoting the text you are commenting on. A native speaker must sign off before upload.
Skills 6–10

Ads

Spend less to rank higher.

06Search-Term Clean-up

SKILL 6: SEARCH-TERM CLEAN-UP

Attach: search term report (30-60 days). Target ACOS: [e.g. 25%]. Brand terms to protect: [ ]

Rules: HARVEST = 2+ orders and ACOS below target. NEGATE = 0 orders and 12+ clicks. BID DOWN = ACOS above 1.5x target with 1+ order. Everything else = WATCH.
Never negate a term containing my brand. Flag it instead.
Show spend in each group and the % of spend in NEGATE. Then 3 tables sorted by spend: Term | Clicks | Spend | Orders | ACOS | Action.
List repeated words in NEGATE terms (e.g. "free", "wholesale") as phrase-negative ideas.

07Bid Check

SKILL 7: BID CHECK

Attach: keyword or targeting report (30 days) with keyword, match type, bid, clicks, spend, orders, sales.
Target ACOS: [ ]

For each keyword with 10+ clicks: suggested bid = current CPC x (target ACOS / actual ACOS).
Cap any change at +/-30% per week. Do not raise bids on keywords with 0 orders.
Table: Keyword | Match | Current bid | Actual ACOS | Suggested bid | Change %.
Flag keywords with too few clicks to judge. Remind me to check that top-of-search placement is not driving the cost.

08Share of Search

SKILL 8: SHARE OF SEARCH

I searched my top keywords and noted the first page. Paste for each keyword: position 1 to 20, brand, sponsored or organic. (Or attach screenshots.)
My brand: [ ]   Keywords: [5-10]

For each keyword: my organic slots, my sponsored slots, my share of page 1 (%), and the brand with the most slots.
Summary table sorted by my weakest share.
Say which keywords to fix with listing content (organic gap) and which with ads (sponsored gap).
Use only what I pasted. Search results change by location and time, so treat this as a snapshot.

09Deal Calendar

SKILL 9: DEAL CALENDAR

Build my promotion calendar for the next [8] weeks.
Paste: platform events and dates I know of, my SKUs with normal margin %, stock on hand, and the max discount I accept per SKU.

Table by week: Week | Event | SKUs to put on deal | Discount | Why | Stock check (enough cover?) | Margin after deal.
Rules: no SKU on deal two weeks in a row unless I say so; no deal on SKUs with under 21 days of cover; keep each SKU's margin above my minimum.
Use only dates I give you. List event dates I should confirm with the platform.

10Badge Check

SKILL 10: BADGE CHECK

For my product: [name, platform]
Paste: rating, review count, price vs my usual price, delivery promise (e.g. Prime, same-day), stock status, return rate if known, and the badges I see on the top 3 listings in my category.

For each badge I see in my category: what it usually depends on (sales, rating, price, delivery, a programme I join), what I control, what the platform decides, and my gap based on my data.
Table: Badge | Depends on | I control? | My gap | Next action.
Badge rules are not always public. Tell me which points to confirm in the platform's help pages.
Skills 11–15

Stock & price

Never run out. Never sell at a loss.

11Days of Cover

SKILL 11: DAYS OF COVER

Attach: SKU, units on hand, units sold last 7 days, units sold last 28 days, units on the way.
Lead time: [days]   Target cover: [days]   Event uplift (optional): [e.g. 2.5x from a sale date]

Daily sales = higher of (7-day / 7) and (28-day / 28). Days of cover = on hand / daily sales.
Status: OUT, REORDER NOW (cover at or below lead time), REORDER SOON (below target), OK, OVERSTOCK (over 3x target).
Reorder qty = target days x daily sales - on hand - on the way (never below 0).
Table sorted by urgency. If a column is missing, stop and ask. Never estimate stock.

12Dark-Store OOS

SKILL 12: DARK-STORE OUT-OF-STOCK

Attach: quick-commerce stock report by dark store or city: SKU, location, on hand, units sold last 7 days.
App: [e.g. Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart]   My top SKUs: [list]

Find: locations with 0 stock on a top SKU; locations with under 3 days of cover; locations with stock but 0 sales (listing or visibility problem).
Tables: OUT now | At risk in 3 days | Stock but no sales. Columns: SKU | Location | On hand | Daily sales | Days of cover.
Rank the cities or clusters with the most gaps. Suggest which to raise with the app's category team first.

13Price Parity

SKILL 13: PRICE PARITY

Paste: my SKUs with the selling price on each platform (and on my own website if I have one), the date checked, and my MRP or recommended retail price.

Table: SKU | Platform | Price | vs MRP % | vs lowest platform % | Flag.
Flag: any price more than [5]% below MRP, any platform more than [5]% cheaper than the others, and any price above MRP.
Say which gaps could trigger a price-matching or "lowest price" check on another platform. Tell me to confirm each platform's own policy.
Use only prices I pasted; prices change daily.

14Promo Margin

SKILL 14: PROMO MARGIN

Per SKU, paste: price, GST/VAT rate, landed cost, commission %, fulfilment fee, ad spend % of sales, planned discount %, who funds it, minimum margin I accept.

Net price = price without GST/VAT. Contribution = net price - commission - fulfilment - ads - landed cost - my share of the discount.
Show normal vs promo: contribution per unit, margin %, and break-even uplift (normal contribution / promo contribution).
Verdict: GO (above my minimum), CAREFUL (positive but below), STOP (zero or negative). For each CAREFUL or STOP, give the smallest change that fixes it.
Never assume a fee. Ask.

15Buy Box Check

SKILL 15: BUY BOX CHECK

Paste for each SKU: who holds the Buy Box (or the default offer) today, the other sellers with their price, delivery speed and fulfilment type, and my own offer.
Platform: [Amazon / Flipkart / other]

Table: SKU | Buy Box holder | My price | Lowest price | My delivery | Best delivery | Likely reason I lost it.
Flag unknown sellers on my brand (possible unauthorised sellers) for me to check, and SKUs where I win only by dropping price.
The exact Buy Box rules are not public. Treat reasons as likely, not certain.
Skills 16–20

Insight

Hear the shopper. Act every week.

16Review Mining

SKILL 16: REVIEW MINING

Paste or attach: star rating and review text (remove customer names).
Product: [ ]   Platform: [ ]

Group complaints (1-3 stars) into themes in the shopper's words, with counts. Tag each with ONE cause: Product, Pack, Listing, Delivery, Price.
Group delights (4-5 stars) the same way.
Output: Top 5 fixes (Fix | Theme | Reviews | Cause | Owner), 2 exact quotes per top theme, 3 headline ideas from delight themes, and questions shoppers ask that my listing doesn't answer.
Under 30 reviews: warn me it is only a first signal. Never write or improve a quote.

17Returns Reasons

SKILL 17: RETURNS REASONS

Attach: returns report with SKU, return reason (code and customer comment), date, and units sold per SKU in the same period.

Return rate per SKU = returns / units sold. Flag SKUs above [3]%.
Group reasons into: Damaged in transit, Not as described, Wrong item, Quality, Changed mind, Other.
Table: SKU | Units sold | Returns | Rate | Top reason | What to fix (pack, listing, warehouse, product).
For "Not as described", say which line or image on the listing may be causing it.

18Competitor Shelf

SKILL 18: COMPETITOR SHELF

Paste the top 5 listings for my main keyword: title, price, pack size, rating, review count, number of images, badges, and delivery promise. Add my own listing the same way.
Keyword: [ ]   Platform: [ ]

Table comparing all 6, with price per 100 g or ml (or per unit).
Then: where I win, where I lose, and the 3 changes that would close the biggest gaps (content, price per unit, pack size, delivery, reviews).
Use only what I pasted. Do not guess sales or rank.

19Launch Checklist

SKILL 19: LAUNCH CHECKLIST

New product: [name, category, pack size]   Platforms: [ ]   Launch date: [ ]   Market: [India / GCC]

Build a launch checklist in 4 phases: T-30, T-14, T-7, launch week.
Cover: listing (title, bullets, images, A+), registrations and label checks for the market, stock and inbound shipments, price and launch offer, ads (budget, keywords), reviews programme if the platform offers one, quick-commerce listing and dark-store allocation.
Table: Phase | Task | Owner | Due date | Done?
Mark tasks that depend on a platform or legal approval. Tell me to confirm current requirements.

20Weekly Readout

SKILL 20: WEEKLY READOUT

Paste this week and last week by platform: sales, units, orders, ad spend, ad sales, sessions (if any), out-of-stock SKUs. Add target and what happened (deals, price changes, stock issues).

One page: 1-sentence headline; scorecard (Metric | This week | Last week | Change | vs target); the 3 biggest changes and why (say "data shows" or "likely, to confirm"); risks for next 7 days; max 5 actions (Action | Owner | By when); "Your call" decisions.
ACOS = ad spend / ad sales. TACOS = ad spend / total sales. Under 300 words outside the tables.
What stays with you

The skills read, count and draft. A person decides.

  • Claims on listings and images must be true and provable.
  • Money: bids, prices and deals change only when you change them. Check the top rows against your source report.
  • Platform rules change. Confirm fees, image rules and badge rules in each platform's help pages.
  • Data: remove customer details before pasting, and follow your company's AI policy.
